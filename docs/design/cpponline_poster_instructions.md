Poster Requirements

This page will give you a quick overview of what you can present in the poster session along with instructions on how to send your material.

Gather Town
Gather Town is a fully interactive virtual interaction platform, in the style of a retro video game. Users can explore an interactive 2d world through a digital avatar. Users view the space as a 2d top-down map where they can move their character around using keyboard controls. Users can interact with each other through proximity-based video and audio chat, as well as interact with various objects and features within the virtual world.

Poster Booths
All of the posters will be presented in Gather Town in our poster room. Each poster will have its own private booth/conversation area, with your material embedded in an interactive object. Attendees will be able to view your material by interacting with the object in your booth (using the ‘X’ key) and can converse with the presenter and other attendees through video chat, within the private booth space.

Each booth provides a private video chat with anyone else within the booth area. Your poster materials will remain embedded in the booth for the duration of the event, so that attendees can view the materials at any time. There will also be a dedicated period of time where we will ask you to be available in the booth, to discuss your poster with people who are interested. You will have provided these times to us through the form on the main Poster information page, which we will then have published to attendees. You can attend the booth as much or as little as you wish (but we ask you do least the 30 minute session that you have been assigned to answer any questions that attendees might have).

During the live interaction session, you can also share your screen with those in the booth (like a mini presentation). You can play video, show web pages or open other materials to present inside of the booth, to any attendees within the space. This is also a good way to do a ‘synced’ viewing of any video or audio materials (where all attendees are listening/viewing at the same time).

Each of your posters will be represented as an interactive object that attendees will be able to walk up to and interact with. This object will be customized to represent the type of content you submit, images will be represented as posters on a billboard, videos as a Tv screen and links as a computer station. Multiple attendees can access the embedded content at the same time, but it's important to note that any video material will not be played in sync to everyone watching (each viewing session is unique to each attendee). Interacting with the object will load or trigger content or actions. Objects can also be interacted with whilst in conversation with other people, allowing booth staff to discuss embedded content with the attendee. Object artwork will be selected to best represent the content embedded within it, so a video will appear in an interactive tv screen, a software demo could be embedded in an interactive computer or laptop, and a website with a sign up form might be displayed inside a clipboard.

You can try this for yourself by viewing the posters from a previous event we have run

Types Of Poster
For your poster, you can submit up to 4 different types of material.

Video - A link to a YouTube, Vimeo or other cloud hosted video. Or an .mp4 file that we can upload to our YouTube channel.
Any video that is 1920x1080 resolution (higher resolution is fine too)
If you are recording your own video and need guidance, please see the accompanying information that you received in your email.
Image - An image that attendees can view.
Please note the image must be:
in .png,  .jpg, .webp format
at least 1000px for the width (although we would recommend 1920px)
at least 600px for the height
We highly recommend creating a poster image in landscape format (4:3 or 16:9), as opposed to portrait format as the viewers will see the image on their monitors.
max 3MB in size - if you need to upload something larger than this, please embed on a webpage or blog, or provide a download link and we will add it as a ‘link’ (see below) which opens in a new tab.

Embedded Webpage - An interactive webpage that loads within a pop up panel inside of the virtual venue. Attendees can view the page whilst interacting with the author at the same time.
Web pages must be embeddable for this to work. To test/check if a webpage will embed, click here. This page also provides information about what to do if your page doesn’t work.
If you need to show a poster with multiple videos, images and/or written content, then this is usually the most effective way of achieveing this, as it's the only method that will allow you to access multiple types of content within a single interface and using a single Gather Town object.

Text Link/Webpage - A link to an external web page or blog or document (PDF for example). When the user then interacts with the object, they will then click on a web address that will either load the link in a new tab or download a document.
Due to text links opening in a new tab, the attendee is not able to interact with the author while viewing the page content, unless they have two monitors and keep the Gather Town window active during the interaction.
Audio - To present audio only material, we suggest either linking to a webpage or audio host (soundcloud for example) or uploading the audio to YouTube or another platform as an audio only video that we can link to (in the same way as the video option above)
You can provide multiple items to be included in the booth, up to a maximum of 3. If you need to show additional examples or media, we suggest using a web page to collate these together so it can be served in a single object.

Poster Instructions
[toc]

Thanks for contributing to C++Online.

This webpage is for anyone who is exhibiting a poster at the conference.

In your sessions, you will present your material in Gather Town at your booth. Your booths will also be available for attendees to look at anytime during the conference

These pages will provide more information on what to expect on the day along with any other important information you need to know.

Gather Town - The Virtual Venue
Gather Town is a fully interactive virtual interaction platform, in the style of a retro video game. Users can explore an interactive 2d world through a digital avatar. Users view the space as a 2d top-down map where they can move their character around using keyboard controls. Users can interact with each other through proximity-based video and audio chat, as well as interact with various objects and features within the virtual world.

Your posters will be represented as booths within the C++Online virtual venue. More information on how your booth will look and work is provided as part of these instructions

Please consider checking out our onboarding space which takes you through the basics in using Gather Town

Go To Onboarding Room


Important Steps
Please complete these steps as soon as you can

C++Online Discord Server - Please join our Discord server
Poster Requirements & Upload - Please provide your poster materials by the 28th February. Once you have provided your material, we will then add the materials you provided to your booth.
Gather Town Instructions - Please read through and have them on hand for the tech check and the conference session
Poster Review
Once your booth has been added, we will invite you to review your poster in the Gather Town venue. Please use this opportunity to check that any materials for your poster have been added correctly.

Please also check that your webcam and microphone work within Gather Town as well. When joining the Gather Town space, there are controls to test your webcam and microphone. If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us via the Discord server so we can arrange a quick technical check/debug session with you.

Please look out for an email inviting you to join the Gather Town space. If you do not receive this invitation by the 9th March, then please let us know in Discord. If you also need any assistance either with your poster or with testing your webcam and microphone, please get in touch with us on the C++Online Discord Server.

Poster Sessions
While attendees will be able to access your poster at any time during the conference, attendees find it much more engaging when the author of the poster is available to discuss and answer questions.

All poster presenters will be asked to be available for at least one 30 minute poster session, where there will be an opportunity to present your poster to interested attendees. All posters will be accessible simultaneously allowing attendees to move from one poster to another to see a range of content and engage with the different presenters/authors throughout the session/s. Poster presenters can engage attendees through video chat only or share their screen, slides or other sources into the poster booth for those in attendance to see and/or hear. We recommend keeping any presentation material to around 5-10 minutes at most, to allow for multiple groups of attendees to join at different points throughout the 30 minute session to engage with you and view the embedded materials. Attendees will also be able to view your materials simultaneously whilst you discuss the materials they are viewing.

If your poster is more experiential, then you can simply be on hand to answer any questions attendees might have once they have finished viewing.

You can use the poster booth and time available as you wish to engage attendees regarding your work/poster, but please remember to keep all content educational and non-commercial. Posters are not an opportunity to promote a commercial product or service. Also keep in mind that attendees will be free to move from booth to booth, so consider the way any additional content is structured or presented, to allow for more than one group to enjoy the material.

With this in mind, can you please click the button below and fill out a short form to confirm when you will be able to attend your booth. If you are not available during the times specified then that is fine but please fill out the form to indicate this.

We will then publish these times both on the C++Online Schedule and on your booth in Gather Town.

Finally, please arrive at your assigned booth 5 minutes before you are due to start in case you need any assistance

This web page will provide you with instructions on how to present your poster in the Gather Town Virtual Venue

Presenting A Poster
To present your poster, you will be given a booth. Typically your booth will look similar to the following:



 

Private Conversation Space - If any attendees enter into your private conversation space, then they will join a private conversation with you and anyone else who is in your space.
Object - Delegates will interact with your object in your booth to view your poster/demo material. They This will be explained in more detail shortly.
Public Conversation Space - Anyone who is here will not be able to see or hear you regardless of how close you are to them UNLESS you also step outside of your private conversation space.
Each booth will have your name underneath so that you can tell which booth is yours.

To see first hand how your booth will work, please visit our demo space which has posters from a previous event we have run.

Go To Demo Space

Objects
Your poster material will typically be represented in your booth by up to 4 different types of objects depending on what type of material you are submitting.

Video
If you have submitted any videos (or a link to a video), then your material will be represented through a TV.

Please note that when an attendee interacts with a video, they will load their own instance of the video and it will not be in sync with what you are seeing. So if you need to refer to discuss a specific part of your video, you may want to wait until everyone has finished watching it.

Image
If you are submitting an image (or a link to an image), then your material will be represented through a Poster in a similar way to the example above. When the user interacts with the image, they will then see the full-sized version of your image.

Website Link
If you are submitting a link to a website then your material will be represented through a computer.

Audio
If you have submitted any audio tracks (or a link to an audio track), then your material will be represented through a grammarphone.

Please note that like with a video, when an attendee interacts with a audio, they will load their own instance of the audio and it will not be in sync with what you are seeing. So again if you need to refer to discuss a specific part of your audio, you may want to wait until everyone has finished listening to it.

Presenting Your Booth
While attendees will be able to access your poster at any time during the conference, attendees find it much more engaging when the author of the poster is available to discuss and answer questions.

With this in mind, can you please click the button below and fill out a short form to confirm when you will be able to attend your booth on the Wednesday and Friday. We will then publish these times both on the C++Online Schedule and on your booth in Gather Town. If you are not available during the times specified then that is fine but please fill out the form to indicate this.

Poster Availability Form

Then at the times that you indicated, you will be expected to attend your booths and discuss your material with any attendees who come to your booth. To do this, you will need to:

Connect to Gather Town
Go to your booth and enter the private conversation space of your booth. You will probably want to stand to the side of your object(s).
Turn on your microphone and webcam
During The Session
Once your session has started, attendees will have to come into your private conversation space in order to view your material. At this point you will be able to see and hear these attendees (assuming they have their mic and webcam on) and they will be able to see and hear you, in a similar way to a Zoom call. As part of viewing your material, attendees may want to ask you questions or discuss your poster with you. Once an attendee has finished viewing your material/speaking with you, they will then leave your private conversation space and their connection to you will be cut.

Please note that attendees will be able to enter your booth at any time so what may start out as a simple 1:1 discussion could potentially grow to a larger number. As a result, you may need to do more to control the discussion to ensure that people are not talking over the top of each other.

Screenshare
If at any point you would like to share your screen while discussing your work, then you can do this using the share screen button on the Gather Town control bar.

However, please note that if you think that you will need to screenshare, then we would recommend that you use Chrome as your browser because:

Chrome is currently the only browser where you can share audio with your screen share. This can also be achieved in other browsers but would require you to use software like Loopback or Voicemeeter to route the system audio through your microphone.
Chrome also supports sharing application windows whereas other browsers will force you to share your whole screen.
Moderation
Any attendees who visit your booth will be able to share their webcam, audio and screen share with you. If you are experiencing issues with any attendees, then you do have the option to mute them and/or turn off their video. To do so, hover your mouse over their video box and click on the microphone and/or video camera icons to mute and/or turn off their video for yourself. Please note that they will still be able to see and hear you and everyone else in the conversation will still be able to see and hear them as well.

Please report any disruptions or issues with attendees in the Discord server and we will send someone to assist.
